The Emotion Engine Is Out

A box of author copies, six signed books already gone, and a paperback two days away.

November 23, 2026 Charles Whitham

This one was supposed to go up yesterday. Sorry for being a day late. I think the reason is a good one, though.

The Emotion Engine is out.

The Kindle edition went live today. If you pre-ordered it, it should already be waiting on your device. Thank you for that. Pre-orders matter more than most people realize, and every one of them meant something to me.

The paperback comes out in two days, on Wednesday, November 25.

The Box

Back in October I ordered a box of author copies so I'd have some here in Japan. Then it showed up.

I was just so happy to actually see it. I'd been looking at this cover on a screen for months and I thought I knew what it looked like. I didn't. Holding it, the cover was more beautiful than I had imagined.

There's something about a stack of real books that a file on a computer can't give you. For a long time the story only existed in my notebooks and my head. Now it has weight.

Signed Copies

That box didn't stay full for long. I've already sold six signed copies. Five went in the mail to the US and one stayed here in Japan. I kept one for myself, which I think is allowed.

If you'd like a signed copy, send me a message through the contact form on this site and ask for one. I'll get back to you.

I'm also working on setting up dates for book signings. Nothing is set yet. When it is, I'll post the details here.

Three Books

That makes three. Each one stands on its own, so you can start anywhere. But if you read more than one, you might notice a few things.
